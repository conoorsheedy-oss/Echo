              Settings

              Echo is bring-your-own-keys: it talks straight to Groq (speech-to-text) and Google Gemini (answers) with your own API keys. Both have free tiers.

              Not set

              Answers

              Auto thinks harder only on interview coding, maths, probability, puzzle and design questions (or when screenshots are attached) and stays quick everywhere else, behavioural questions included. Fast always answers quickest; Max always thinks hardest (slower).

              Script gives you sentences to say, one per line. Cues gives you a first line to say, then 3-4 bold keywords to talk around in your own words. Also switchable with the Script/Cues chip next to "Suggested response".

              Where your data goes

              • Audio chunks go to Groq for transcription, under your key.
              • Transcript text, matched knowledge-base snippets and any screenshots you attach go to Google Gemini, under your key.
              • Nothing goes to any Echo server — there isn't one.
              • On Gemini's free tier, Google may use your prompts and responses to improve its products. Use a paid (billing-enabled) key to avoid that.
